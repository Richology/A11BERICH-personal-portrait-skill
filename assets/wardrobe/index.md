# Wardrobe assets

No permanent wardrobe image is currently approved. For each request, label uploaded clothing references as `clothing` and use only garment silhouette, construction, material, color, and explicitly requested logos/text. Do not inherit the model, face, body, pose, scene, lighting, UI, or background from a clothing reference.

When a recurring outfit is approved, copy one clean, useful reference here and add it to `asset-manifest.yaml` with its role and confidence. Avoid near-duplicate angles unless they resolve a genuinely ambiguous garment structure.
